Origin

I cycled 600 kilometres across Sri Lanka for charity (Ride for Ceylon). My legs gave up several times. My mind did not. I crossed the finish line. I would do it again tomorrow without hesitation.

2 years back, I donated my hair

I grew my hair for over a year and donated it to the Little Princess Trust, an organisation that makes wigs for children going through cancer treatment. It is the best thing I have ever done with my hair. It is a reminder that the best use of your time is usually something that helps someone else.

I restored a bike older than me and rode it across Sri Lanka

I bought a thirty-year-old cruiser bike, took it apart, rebuilt it with my own hands, and toured Sri Lanka on it with my wife. Every breakdown was a problem to solve. Every open road was worth it. That trip taught me more about patience, creativity, and teamwork than any management course I have ever done. 

Hiking is my stress buster

I love hiking. Sri Lanka has extraordinary trails and I have walked more of them than I can count. There is something about starting at the bottom of a mountain and reaching the top that never gets old. Same feeling as watching a project go live.
